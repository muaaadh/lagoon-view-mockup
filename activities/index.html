Activities

Sandbanks, reefs and open water, a short boat ride away.

Snorkeling

Guided trips of one to two hours. The house reef costs nothing extra.

  • Turtle reef
  • Manta rays
  • Nurse sharks at Gangehi
  • Mathiveri wreck
  • House reef at night

Sandbanks and islands

Picnic on the sand, or spend a day on a local island.

  • Mathiveri Sandbank
  • Dhinnolhu Sandbank
  • Mathiveri, Feridhoo and Maalhos
  • Day visits to Nika and Gangehi

Manta and dolphin search

One to two hours within a 10 mile radius. Two people minimum.

  • Manta ray search
  • Dolphin search
  • Lobster hunt

Water sports

Twenty-minute rides, plus kayaks and paddleboards.

  • Jet-ski
  • Fun tube
  • Wakeboard
  • Windsurf
  • Kayak and paddleboard

Fishing

Your catch can go straight to the kitchen.

  • Sunset fishing, 2 hours
  • Deep bottom fishing, 2 hours
  • Jigging and popping, 3 hours
  • Big game, 4 hours

Cruises

One to two hours, with sandbank stops and dolphin spotting.

  • Sunset cruise
  • Celebration cruise

Spa and gym

Massages, facials, couple treatments and yoga. Open 11am to 11pm.

A compact fitness studio for daily workouts.
