Good to know

When is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 2pm and check-out at 11am.

Can I wear a bikini?

Yes, at the hotel and on our private beach. Outside the hotel, please dress modestly with shoulders and knees covered.

Is alcohol available?

Alcohol is not allowed on local islands. Nika and Gangehi resorts are a five-minute boat ride away if you would like a drink.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, Wi-Fi is free.

How can I pay?

USD, Euro, British Pounds or card. Local shops usually prefer cash.

Is there a dress code?

Not at the hotel, but please cover swimwear in the restaurant.
